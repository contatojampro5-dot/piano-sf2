Sons gratuitos do Piano SF2
===========================

Todos os sons vêm do projeto FreePats (https://freepats.zenvoid.org), montados por Roberto (zenvoid.org).
Foram só juntados num arquivo (PacoteIgreja.sf2) e renomeados; o som é o original.

PacoteIgreja.sf2 — domínio público (CC0 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
  Piano de Armario ......... Upright Piano KW (FreePats)
  Piano Eletrico (4) ....... FM Piano 1 (FreePats)
  Orgao de Igreja .......... Church Organ Emulation (FreePats)
  Orgao Hammond ............ Drawbar Organ Emulation (FreePats)
  Pad Coral ................ Synth Pad Choir (FreePats)
  Pad Cordas Arco .......... Synth Pad Bowed (FreePats)
  Pad New Age .............. New Age (FreePats)
  Pad Varredura ............ Sweep Pad (FreePats)
  Cordas ................... Synth Strings 1 (FreePats)
  Harpa .................... Concert Harp (FreePats)
  Sinos .................... Tubular Bells (FreePats)

PianoSalamander.sf2 — Salamander Grand Piano V3 (Yamaha C5), criado por Alexander Holm,
versão SF2 do FreePats. Licença Creative Commons Atribuição 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
